# Specialist engine outputs

Some of the evidence has been pre-processed by specialised engines (filtering, duplicate detection, ranking, comparable-adjusted valuation). Their outputs appear under `specialist_outputs` in the evidence, with their inputs, parameters and caveats. Use them as you would a competent analyst's working: rely on them for the mechanical work, check them where they matter, and say when you disagree or when their caveats affect the decision. Do not redo the whole computation by hand; spend your effort on judgement, trade-offs, missing evidence and the next action.
